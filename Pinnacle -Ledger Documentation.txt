Financial Management System - Documentation
Modules Overview
• Dashboard: Get a quick overview of funds, budgets, transactions, and project performance.
• Donors: Register and manage donor information. Filter and export donor lists.
• Funds: Record incoming funds, link them to donors, programs, and projects. Track restricted/unrestricted funds.
• Budgets: Allocate budgets to programs, projects, and activities. Ensure budgets do not exceed available funds.
• Transactions: Record both fund receipts and expenses. Expenses are validated against allocated budgets.
• Categories & Subcategories: Organize expense types for detailed reporting and filtering.
• Programs & Administration: Manage organizational programs and administrative units.
• Projects & Activities: Define projects under programs and break them down into activities.
• Reports: Generate and export financial reports by year/month, including charts and summaries.
Typical Workflow for Users
• Set Up Programs and Administrators
o Begin by adding your Programs or Administrative Units—the major areas your organization works in.
o Include who manages each program for accountability.
• Register Donors
o Add details of Donors, including their type (e.g. individual, organization) and contact information.
o This will help track the source of funds later.
• Organize Expense Types
o Create Categories (like Office, Transport, Training) and Subcategories (e.g. Fuel, Stationery).
o This helps classify how money is spent.
• Create Projects and Activities
o Under each Program, define Projects (like "Youth Training" or "Health Campaign").
o Break projects into specific Activities (e.g. "Training Session 1", "Buying Equipment").
• Add Incoming Funds
o When funds are received, record them under Funds and link them to the relevant Donor, Program, and Project.
o Mention if the fund is restricted (for specific use) or unrestricted.
• Allocate Budgets
o From the available funds, assign Budgets to specific Projects and Activities.
o Ensure budgets align with planned work and available money.
• Record Financial Transactions
o Log all Transactions, both income and expenses.
o Link each transaction to a Program, Project, Activity, and Expense Type (Category/Subcategory).
o Add descriptions and references for clarity and tracking.
• Monitor and Report
o Use the Reports section to view summaries of funds, expenses, and budgets.
o Filter by year, Month.
o Generate reports to PDF.
Remind: The Export option on all tables have to two ways to export either Excel or PDF.
